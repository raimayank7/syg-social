Quick replies for a travel agent and taxi

Ready messages with shortcut names, to save in the WhatsApp Business app.

Travel customers want a quote for a route and date. Quick replies that ask for exactly those details make quotes fast and correct.

Always send the quote in writing with what is and is not included.

  • /ask

    To quote, please tell us: where from and to, dates, number of people and any special needs.

  • /taxi

    A taxi from ____ to ____ on ____ costs Rs ____ for ____ seats. Tolls and parking are ____.

  • /package

    Our ____ package for ____ people for ____ days is Rs ____. It includes ____. It does not include ____.

  • /confirm

    Your booking is confirmed for ____. The driver is ____ (phone ____). Pick-up is at ____.

  • /docs

    Please carry a photo ID and ____. Tickets will be sent on ____.

  • /change

    We can change the date until ____. After that, a charge of Rs ____ applies.

  • /safe

    Please share your live location with a family member for the journey.

  • /thanks

    Thank you for travelling with us. We hope you had a good trip.

Using these replies

Tap Copy, then in the WhatsApp Business app open Tools, Quick replies, add a reply, paste the text and type the shortcut shown, for example /hours. In a chat, typing / lists your replies. Fill every blank first, and edit the words so that they sound like you. Read how quick replies work and an order workflow.

Questions people ask

How do I save these in WhatsApp Business?

In the app open Tools, then Quick replies, add a new one, paste the message and type the shortcut shown. See the quick replies guide. The app allows 50 stored replies, so keep your best ones.

What do the blanks (____) mean?

They show where to put your own detail: a price, a time, an address. Fill them before you save so you do not have to edit each time.
