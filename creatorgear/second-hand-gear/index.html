Buying second-hand gear safely

What is safe to buy used (stands, lights, reflectors), what is risky (batteries, storage cards) and how to check an item in person.

Used gear can save money, if you choose the right things and check them.

Usually fine used

  • Tripods and stands (check legs and locks).
  • Reflectors and backdrops.
  • Lights (check the switch and the brightness steps).
  • Headphones (check both sides and the cable).

Riskier used

  • Batteries and power banks: they wear out and can be unsafe.
  • Memory cards and drives: they can fail without warning.
  • Gimbals: motors wear out.
  • Anything that has been in water.

Check in person

  1. Meet in a public place in daylight.
  2. Switch it on and use it for a few minutes.
  3. Ask for the original box and the invoice.
  4. Check that the serial number matches and the item is not reported stolen, if there is a way to check.
  5. Do not pay in advance to a stranger.
