A starter kit for sellers photographing products

For a shop or home business that photographs and films products for posts.

Light and background matter most for products. A plain backdrop, soft light from the side and a steady tripod give clean pictures. Add a reflector to fill shadows.

What is in it

  • Backdrop and background
  • LED panel light and softbox
  • Tripod
  • Reflector and diffuser

Each item has its own guide with what to check and a search link. Buy them in the order shown, and test each one before you add the next. Read what to buy first and the buying checklist.
