Reflector and diffuser

A folding disc that bounces light onto a face or product or softens a strong light.

Who needs it

Photographers and creators who film near a window or in sun.

What to check before you buy

  • Size: bigger covers more but is harder to hold.
  • Surfaces: white to soften, silver to brighten, gold to warm.
  • A cover that folds into a small bag.
  • Strength of the frame.
  • Whether it comes with a holder.

When to skip it

A white board or cloth works as a free reflector.

A tip

Hold it opposite the window and move it slowly until the shadows soften.
