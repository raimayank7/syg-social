A phone for making videos

What to look for in a phone if you make videos: camera, storage, battery and sound.

Who needs it

Anyone buying or upgrading a phone to make content.

What to check before you buy

  • Storage: video files are big; avoid the smallest option.
  • The camera at night and in moving shots; watch review videos.
  • Battery life with the screen on.
  • A microphone jack or adapter support.
  • Updates and service nearby.

When to skip it

If your current phone films clearly and has enough storage, upgrade your light and sound first.

A tip

Spend on light and sound before the phone. A good clip-on mic and a window beat a new camera.
