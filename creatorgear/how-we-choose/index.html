How we choose and write these guides

How this site decides what to explain, why it gives no prices or model names, how affiliate links work and how to check a product yourself.

Most gear sites tell you what to buy. We tell you what to check, because the best product changes every few months and depends on your phone, your room and your budget.

What we do

  • Explain what each item does and who really needs it.
  • List what to check on the product page before you buy.
  • Say when you can skip the item.
  • Give one simple test to run when it arrives.

What we do not do

  • We do not quote prices, because they change daily.
  • We do not name a best model, because we do not test products.
  • We do not accept payment for reviews or placement.

Affiliate links

We take part in the Amazon Associates programme. The links on this site open a search on Amazon.in with our tracking tag. If you buy something after using one, we may earn a commission, and you pay nothing extra. We would give the same advice without the links. If you prefer, search for the same words in any shop.

Check a product yourself

  1. Read the description for the plug, size, weight and what is in the box.
  2. Read recent reviews, especially the middling ones, which are often the most honest.
  3. Look at who is selling and whether you get a proper invoice and a warranty.
  4. Test it as soon as it arrives and return it within the shop's return window if it fails.
