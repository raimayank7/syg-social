Clip-on microphone

A small microphone that clips to your shirt and plugs into your phone or camera, so your voice is clear even when you stand away from the phone.

Who needs it

Anyone who talks in videos: teachers, shop owners, cooks, vloggers. Sound is the first thing viewers judge.

What to check before you buy

  • The plug: check whether your phone needs a USB-C, Lightning or headphone-style connection, and buy the right one or an adapter.
  • The cable length: a longer cable lets you stand further back.
  • Wired or wireless: wired is cheaper and never runs out of battery; wireless gives freedom but needs charging.
  • A wind cover (a small fur or foam cap) if you film outdoors.
  • Whether it works with the camera app of your phone: test before an important shoot.

When to skip it

If you only record quiet voice notes at home, your phone's own microphone may be enough.

A tip

Record ten seconds with the new microphone and with the phone alone. If you cannot hear a clear difference, return it.
