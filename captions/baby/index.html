Baby and kids captions

For little ones and proud parents.

Parents and relatives post baby photos with joy and no filter. A short, warm line works. Keep personal details such as full name, school and exact location out of the caption.

Use them for a baby's first smile, a birthday, a school day or a playground photo.

  • Tiny hands, huge love.

  • Our world got softer today.

  • Every day with you is a new first.

  • Small feet, big dreams.

  • The best thing we ever made.

  • You make ordinary days magical.

  • My little sunshine.

  • You smiled and everything else disappeared.

  • First steps, first words, forever memories.

  • A little giggle can fix a whole bad day.

  • Sleepy cuddles are the best.

  • Growing too fast, loved too much.

  • Mom and Dad's whole heart in one little person.

  • Messy hair, messy hands, happy heart.

  • Every child is a new story to read.

  • You are our favourite chapter.

  • Cheeks I could kiss all day.

  • Play, laugh, nap, repeat.

  • You are still learning the world, and I am learning you.

  • Home feels complete with you in it.

Making them your own

Think before you post a child's photo publicly. A private or close-friends list is safer for children.

Each button copies the line exactly. The WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp with the line ready to send to a chat or to set as a status.

Questions people ask

Can I change these captions?

Yes. Change a word or add a detail of your own, such as a place or a name, so that the line fits your photo.

Are these captions free to use?

Yes, for your own posts and status. Please do not copy the whole page to another site.
