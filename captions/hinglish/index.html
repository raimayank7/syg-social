Hinglish captions

Roman-letter Hindi and English mixed, the way we talk.

Hinglish is how many people really talk and write: Hindi words in English letters, with English words mixed in. These captions are short, relaxed and ready for Instagram and WhatsApp status.

Use them for photos with friends, a casual day, a selfie or a funny post.

  • Zindagi ek hi hai, thoda enjoy kar lo.

  • Mood on hai, light on hai, bas camera ka intezaar tha.

  • Chai ho, dost ho, aur kya chahiye.

  • Aaj ka plan: kuch nahi. Aur woh bhi pura karna hai.

  • Dil ki suno, par pehle chai pi lo.

  • Sab theek hai, bas thoda late hai.

  • Apna time aayega, abhi chai ka time hai.

  • Simple rehna hi sabse badi baat hai.

  • Yaar, tum ho toh sab kuch sahi lagta hai.

  • Thodi masti, thoda pyaar, bahut saari chai.

  • Bas apni dhun mein.

  • Kal ki fikar kal karenge, aaj photo kheench lo.

  • Natural look, natural smile.

  • Sab kuch milega, bas hausla rakho.

  • Shant dil, mast zindagi.

  • Happy rehna hi apna swag hai.

  • Thoda sa nakhra, thoda sa pyaar.

  • Dosti pakki, baatein lambi.

  • Aaj ka din bas khushi ke naam.

  • Jo hai, wohi sahi hai.

Making them your own

Keep the spelling as you speak. Nobody counts the extra a in "yaar".

Each button copies the line exactly. The WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp with the line ready to send to a chat or to set as a status.

Questions people ask

Can I change these captions?

Yes. Change a word or add a detail of your own, such as a place or a name, so that the line fits your photo.

Are these captions free to use?

Yes, for your own posts and status. Please do not copy the whole page to another site.
