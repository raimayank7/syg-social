Self-love captions

Kind words for yourself.

Self-love captions are best when they are gentle, not preachy. They sound like what you would say to a friend who was being too hard on themselves.

Use them for a birthday, a new look, a rest day or a comeback after a hard time.

  • I am doing my best, and that is enough.

  • Be gentle with yourself. You are still learning.

  • I deserve the kindness I give to others.

  • Rest is not lazy. Rest is repair.

  • I am proud of how far I have come.

  • Choosing peace over proving.

  • My worth is not up for debate.

  • Take a deep breath. You are doing okay.

  • I am enough today, as I am.

  • Today I will be kind to myself.

  • I forgive the old me. She did what she could.

  • Healing is not a straight line.

  • Self-care is not selfish.

  • I am a work in progress and that is fine.

  • I make time for me, and it feels good.

  • No one else needs to approve of my joy.

  • I am allowed to take up space.

  • Treat yourself like someone you love.

  • My story is still being written.

  • Today I choose me.

Making them your own

Keep the line short. Self-love is quiet; it does not need ten sentences.

Each button copies the line exactly. The WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp with the line ready to send to a chat or to set as a status.

Questions people ask

Can I change these captions?

Yes. Change a word or add a detail of your own, such as a place or a name, so that the line fits your photo.

Are these captions free to use?

Yes, for your own posts and status. Please do not copy the whole page to another site.
