Eid captions

For Eid mornings and family tables.

Eid captions should be warm and respectful. A greeting, a thought of gratitude and a mention of family are enough.

Use them for an Eid-ul-Fitr or Eid-ul-Adha photo, a family table or a new outfit.

  • Eid Mubarak. May your home be full of joy and peace.

  • New clothes, warm hugs and sweet sevaiyan.

  • Thankful for family, health and this blessed day.

  • Eid is the day to share, so share a smile.

  • May your prayers be answered and your table be full.

  • Together with family is the best Eid gift.

  • Peace, blessings and good food.

  • Eid greetings to everyone near and far.

  • Dressed up, sweets in hand, heart full.

  • May this Eid bring kindness to every home.

  • A day for gratitude, hugs and meeting everyone.

  • Joy shared is joy doubled.

  • Eid morning has its own special light.

  • Wishing you a day of love and light.

  • Feeling grateful this Eid.

  • Sevaiyan, biryani, family: complete.

  • May we all be kinder after this Eid.

  • Eid with the ones who matter most.

  • Smiles are the best Eidi.

  • Eid Mubarak to you and your family.

Making them your own

Use the greeting your family uses. Many people write "Eid Mubarak" and nothing more.

Each button copies the line exactly. The WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp with the line ready to send to a chat or to set as a status.

Questions people ask

Can I change these captions?

Yes. Change a word or add a detail of your own, such as a place or a name, so that the line fits your photo.

Are these captions free to use?

Yes, for your own posts and status. Please do not copy the whole page to another site.
