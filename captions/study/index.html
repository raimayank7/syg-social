Study and exams captions

For a student desk or a result day.

Student posts are about tired eyes, big goals and a pile of notes. A caption that is honest and light helps friends feel they are not alone.

Use them for exam time, a result, a farewell, a degree photo or a library selfie.

  • Notes, tea and a lot of hope.

  • Exams are temporary, knowledge stays.

  • One more chapter, then I will rest.

  • Syllabus is long, but so is my patience.

  • The result is not the whole story.

  • Sleep or study? I chose both badly.

  • Library vibes and big dreams.

  • Keep going. You are closer than you think.

  • Degree done. Now for the next chapter.

  • Late-night study, early-morning panic.

  • Passing marks, passing thoughts, passing time.

  • Hard work pays off, they say. I am waiting for the payment.

  • Studying hard so that future me can relax.

  • Books are my favourite escape.

  • Thank you, teachers, for believing when I did not.

  • Not the topper, but I did my best.

  • Revising with a friend makes it half as hard.

  • I am not afraid of exams. I am afraid of the syllabus.

  • Graduated. Now what? Chai first.

  • Small steps every day lead to big results.

Making them your own

Add the exam or course name if you want friends to wish you luck.

Each button copies the line exactly. The WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp with the line ready to send to a chat or to set as a status.

Questions people ask

Can I change these captions?

Yes. Change a word or add a detail of your own, such as a place or a name, so that the line fits your photo.

Are these captions free to use?

Yes, for your own posts and status. Please do not copy the whole page to another site.
