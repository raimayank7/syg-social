Birthday captions

For your own birthday post.

On your own birthday, people expect a short thank-you and a happy photo. A caption that is humble and a little funny makes everyone feel welcome in the comments.

Use these for your own birthday story or post. For wishes to send to someone else, see the wishes site.

  • One more year of me. Bring cake.

  • Another trip around the sun, and I enjoyed the ride.

  • Thank you for every wish. It means a lot.

  • Age is just a number, and mine is getting bigger.

  • Feeling grateful for the year gone and ready for the year ahead.

  • Cake is the main character today.

  • Still young at heart, tired at knees.

  • Today I am officially a year wiser. Terms and conditions apply.

  • Another year, another chance to grow.

  • Surrounded by love and snacks. Perfect.

  • New age, same me, more cake.

  • Thank you for making this day so special.

  • A little older, a lot happier.

  • My birthday wish is already granted: all of you.

  • Today the world gets to celebrate me, and I accept.

  • Counting blessings, not candles.

  • Time flies, memories stay.

  • Candles lit, wish made, ready for the year.

  • Cheers to a new chapter.

  • I am not getting older. I am getting more experienced.

Making them your own

Say thank you to the people who wished you. It is the one line that is never out of place.

Each button copies the line exactly. The WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp with the line ready to send to a chat or to set as a status.

Questions people ask

Can I change these captions?

Yes. Change a word or add a detail of your own, such as a place or a name, so that the line fits your photo.

Are these captions free to use?

Yes, for your own posts and status. Please do not copy the whole page to another site.
