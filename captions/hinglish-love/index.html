Hinglish love captions

Soft Roman Hindi for someone special.

Love sounds natural in Hinglish, because that is how people really flirt and tease. These lines are gentle and a little playful, nothing heavy.

Use them for a couple photo, an anniversary or a small thank-you.

  • Tum ho toh har din special hai.

  • Chai ke saath tumhari baatein, aur kya chahiye.

  • Tumhare saath waqt ka pata hi nahi chalta.

  • Dil ko sukoon tumhare paas aake milta hai.

  • Sab se pyaara tohfa tumhara saath hai.

  • Tum mere liye ghar jaise ho.

  • Ek hi dil tha, tumhe de diya.

  • Tumhari muskurahat meri sabse achhi wajah hai.

  • Saath chalte rahenge, chahe raasta kuch bhi ho.

  • Mere liye tum hi sab kuch ho.

  • Tumhare saath chhoti si baat bhi yaadgaar ho jaati hai.

  • Sabse achha hissa mera din ka, tum ho.

  • Tum ho toh mehfil, tum nahi toh khaali.

  • Mujhe tumse milke kabhi bore nahi hua.

  • Tum meri aadat nahi, meri zaroorat ho.

  • Bas tum aur tumhari baatein.

  • Pyaar mein do log bhi puri duniya lagte hain.

  • Tumhe dekhke din ban jaata hai.

  • Humesha aise hi hasna.

  • Tum meri sabse achhi kahani ho.

Making them your own

Use the name or nickname you really use. It makes the line more personal than any poem.

Each button copies the line exactly. The WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp with the line ready to send to a chat or to set as a status.

Questions people ask

Can I change these captions?

Yes. Change a word or add a detail of your own, such as a place or a name, so that the line fits your photo.

Are these captions free to use?

Yes, for your own posts and status. Please do not copy the whole page to another site.
