Raksha Bandhan captions

A thread of love between siblings.

Raksha Bandhan is about a thread and a promise. Captions for the day should be warm and a little teasing, like the festival itself.

Use them for a rakhi photo, a sweet box, a video call between far-away siblings or a family photo.

  • A thread, a promise and a lifetime of love.

  • Brother, you are my protector and my favourite fight.

  • Sister, thank you for every rakhi and every secret.

  • Distance cannot stop a rakhi from reaching you.

  • Teasing all year, tying a rakhi today.

  • Rakhi is love tied around a wrist.

  • My brother, my best friend and my bank in emergencies.

  • A sister's blessing is a brother's strength.

  • Sweets, gifts and old stories. Rakhi at home.

  • May we always be there for each other.

  • You are the best gift of my childhood.

  • No matter how old we get, we are still kids at Rakhi.

  • Rakhi by video call, hug by promise.

  • Happy Raksha Bandhan to my forever partner.

  • The best rakhi gift is time together.

  • Bound by love, not by distance.

  • Siblings: the first friends and the last to leave.

  • Tie the thread and the memories come back.

  • You tie the rakhi and I keep the promise.

  • Grateful to have you in my life.

Making them your own

Mention a childhood memory with your brother or sister. It makes the post personal.

Each button copies the line exactly. The WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp with the line ready to send to a chat or to set as a status.

Questions people ask

Can I change these captions?

Yes. Change a word or add a detail of your own, such as a place or a name, so that the line fits your photo.

Are these captions free to use?

Yes, for your own posts and status. Please do not copy the whole page to another site.
