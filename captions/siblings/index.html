Brother and sister captions

For the person who knows all your secrets.

Siblings fight, tease and still stand first in line when you need help. A caption for a brother or sister works best with a little teasing and a little thanks together.

Use them for Raksha Bandhan, Bhai Dooj, a birthday or any family photo of the two of you.

  • My oldest friend and my longest fight.

  • Siblings: the people who annoy you and defend you in the same breath.

  • You know my secrets and you still sit next to me.

  • Born to share a room and fight about the remote.

  • Brother from the same house, partner in every plan.

  • Sister, you are my best friend with free advice.

  • We argue like cats and love like family.

  • A sibling is a friend who cannot leave.

  • No one teases me like you and no one cheers louder.

  • Thank you for sharing the last piece of everything.

  • My partner in crime and in hiding the mess.

  • You were my first friend and you are still the best.

  • No matter how far we are, you are my home.

  • Brother, you are my backup plan and my favourite person.

  • Sister, you are proof that best friends can be born.

  • We grew up sharing everything, even the blame.

  • You are the only one who can annoy me and get away with it.

  • Some of my best memories start with "do not tell Mom".

  • My childhood has your face in every photo.

  • I am lucky to call you mine.

Making them your own

Mix one joke and one thank-you: it feels exactly like how siblings talk.

Each button copies the line exactly. The WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp with the line ready to send to a chat or to set as a status.

Questions people ask

Can I change these captions?

Yes. Change a word or add a detail of your own, such as a place or a name, so that the line fits your photo.

Are these captions free to use?

Yes, for your own posts and status. Please do not copy the whole page to another site.
