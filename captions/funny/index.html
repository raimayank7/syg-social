Funny captions

Light jokes about everyday life.

Funny captions rely on small truths: sleep, food, Mondays, traffic, the phone battery. A line that makes one friend laugh in the comments is worth more than ten clever ones nobody gets.

Use them for casual photos, food, pets and plain days. Keep the joke on yourself, not on another person.

  • I run on tea and optimism. Mostly tea.

  • My bed and I are in a very serious relationship.

  • I am not lazy. I am saving energy for later. Much later.

  • Diet starts tomorrow. It has said that for years.

  • My phone is at two per cent and so am I.

  • Adulting is just googling how to do things and acting calm.

  • I came, I saw, I forgot why I came.

  • Brain: sleep. Alarm: no. Me: five more minutes.

  • I am on a seafood diet. I see food and I eat it.

  • Plan for today: survive, snack, repeat.

  • Proud owner of forty unread messages and zero worries.

  • Why is it always the last charger in the house that stops working?

  • My wallet and I have a trust problem after every sale.

  • Sunday evening wants me to be sad. I refuse. I have snacks.

  • I told my plants to grow. They are on strike.

  • Some people have a gym body. I have a sofa body.

  • Wi-Fi was slow, so I read a book. Never again.

  • I laugh at my own jokes because someone has to.

  • Please do not ask what is in my search history. It is all recipes.

  • Today I am running on caffeine and good intentions.

Making them your own

Read it once out loud. If you would not say it to a friend sitting next to you, change it.

Each button copies the line exactly. The WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp with the line ready to send to a chat or to set as a status.

Questions people ask

Can I change these captions?

Yes. Change a word or add a detail of your own, such as a place or a name, so that the line fits your photo.

Are these captions free to use?

Yes, for your own posts and status. Please do not copy the whole page to another site.
