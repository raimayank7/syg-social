Selfie captions

Short, easy lines for a photo of yourself.

A selfie caption works best when it sounds like you talking, not like a poster. These lines are short on purpose: the photo does most of the work and the caption only adds a smile or a small joke.

Use them for a casual photo, a new profile picture or a story. Pick one that matches your mood that day, then change a word or two so it is yours.

  • Just me, no filter, good light.

  • This is my good side. So is the other one.

  • Caught mid-smile.

  • Today's mood: comfortable.

  • Not posing. Okay, a little.

  • Hair did its own thing today.

  • Practising my serious face. Failed.

  • Selfie because the light was too good to waste.

  • Some days you just look like yourself, and that is enough.

  • Me, myself and a very average Tuesday.

  • The camera and I are friends today.

  • Looking at the lens like it owes me money.

  • One photo, twelve tries.

  • Plain white shirt, big smile.

  • Nothing special. Just happy.

  • If this photo is blurry, I was probably laughing.

  • Eyes open, finally.

  • Proof that I do leave the house.

  • Comfortable in my own skin, finally.

  • New haircut, same me.

Making them your own

If the photo is serious, keep the caption plain. If the photo is silly, let the caption be silly too.

Questions people ask

Can I change these captions?

Yes. Change a word or add a detail of your own, such as a place or a name, so that the line fits your photo.
