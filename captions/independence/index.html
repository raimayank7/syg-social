Independence Day and Republic Day captions

Respectful lines for the tricolour days.

National days call for pride and respect, not for hype. Keep captions simple and dignified, and use the national flag with care.

Use them on 15 August and 26 January for school, office, society or home posts.

  • Proud to be Indian, proud to be free.

  • Saluting everyone who gave us this day.

  • A small flag, a big feeling.

  • Freedom is a gift we must keep with care.

  • The tricolour in the sky, pride in our hearts.

  • Respect the flag, respect each other.

  • Happy Independence Day to everyone.

  • Jai Hind.

  • Unity in diversity is our strength.

  • Thank you to the soldiers who stand for us.

  • The Constitution is our promise to each other.

  • Happy Republic Day. Let us read the Preamble again.

  • May we always honour the freedom we have.

  • Our flag, our pride, our responsibility.

  • Celebrating the spirit of India today.

  • Gratitude for the dreams that built this country.

  • Let us do something good for the country today.

  • A nation is built by everyday kindness.

  • Proud of where we come from.

  • Wishing you a peaceful and proud national day.

Making them your own

Follow the flag code: display the tricolour with respect and never as a costume or a decoration on the ground.

Each button copies the line exactly. The WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp with the line ready to send to a chat or to set as a status.

Questions people ask

Can I change these captions?

Yes. Change a word or add a detail of your own, such as a place or a name, so that the line fits your photo.

Are these captions free to use?

Yes, for your own posts and status. Please do not copy the whole page to another site.
