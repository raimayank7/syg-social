Mother captions

A few honest words for Mom.

A caption for your mother does not need to be long. The most loved ones are specific: a food she makes, a thing she says, a way she waits for you at the door.

Use them on Mother's Day, her birthday, or any day you feel thankful. Add a real photo of the two of you.

  • Her love is the first home I ever had.

  • Mom, you are the reason I know what care looks like.

  • Nobody makes plain dal taste like home the way she does.

  • She says "eat something" the way others say "I love you".

  • Thank you for believing in me before I did.

  • Strong, soft and always on call.

  • Every time I am tired, I remember her hands on my head.

  • My first teacher and still my best.

  • The world has many lessons, but hers are the ones I use.

  • She forgets herself and remembers everyone else.

  • Mom, your prayers walk with me everywhere.

  • Nothing is as comforting as her voice on the phone.

  • She never asked for credit, so I am saying it now: thank you.

  • Her smile is the best medicine I know.

  • Home is wherever Mom is waiting.

  • You gave me roots and then you let me fly.

  • Mom, I hope I become half the person you are.

  • Her cooking is love you can taste.

  • For all the nights you stayed up: thank you.

  • Mom, you are my favourite person.

Making them your own

Add one thing she does that nobody else does. It will make her smile more than any big phrase.

Questions people ask

Can I change these captions?

Yes. Change a word or add a detail of your own, such as a place or a name, so that the line fits your photo.

Are these captions free to use?

Yes, for your own posts and status. Please do not copy the whole page to another site.
