Navratri and Durga Puja captions

For garba nights and pandal visits.

Navratri and Durga Puja bring music, colour and devotion. Captions can be joyful about the dance and respectful about the prayer.

Use them for garba and dandiya, a pandal visit, a fasting day or a family aarti.

  • Nine nights of dance and devotion.

  • Garba tonight, prayers every day.

  • Colours of the day, strength of the goddess.

  • The pandal lights are the prettiest part of the year.

  • Dandiya sticks, bright clothes, big smiles.

  • May Maa bless every home with peace.

  • Dancing in circles, praying in silence.

  • New outfit for every night, same joy.

  • Strength, faith and a lot of footwork.

  • Navratri nights are the best nights.

  • A little prayer, a lot of music.

  • Maa Durga, give us courage and kindness.

  • From fasting to feasting, it is a whole festival.

  • Garba shoes are tired but the heart is not.

  • The drum, the dance and the divine.

  • Thankful for the community that celebrates together.

  • Evening aarti, then the dance floor.

  • Shubh Navratri to all of you.

  • Dhol beats and sweet prasad.

  • Blessings felt, memories made.

Making them your own

Mention the night or the colour of the day. Many people follow the nine colours.

Questions people ask

Yes. Change a word or add a detail of your own, such as a place or a name, so that the line fits your photo.
