How to pick a caption

Open the list that matches your photo, read down it once and choose the line that sounds most like you. Then change a word or add a small detail of your own, such as the place, a name or the food. That small change is what makes a caption feel written, not borrowed. If the photo is serious, choose a plain line; if it is silly, let the caption be silly too.
