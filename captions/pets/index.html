Pets captions

For dogs, cats and other small family.

Pet photos get the most love, and the best captions sound like what the pet would say. A little humour goes a long way.

Use them for a dog walk, a cat nap, a new puppy or an old friend.

  • My dog thinks I am the best thing ever. I agree with my dog.

  • Nap time is the only plan he has.

  • She stole my seat and my heart.

  • Home is where the tail wags.

  • This face asks for biscuits and gets them.

  • Cats do not need approval, but they accept snacks.

  • He walks me, I like to think.

  • Fluffy, soft and in charge of the house.

  • A dog is the only love that asks for nothing but a walk.

  • Tiny paws, big personality.

  • My pet is a better friend than half my phone contacts.

  • Rainy day, sleepy cat, perfect.

  • If you see this face, you have to give a treat. Rules.

  • She just looked at me. I gave her my dinner.

  • Adopt, do not shop. Love, do not leave.

  • Our little family is complete.

  • Zoomies at six in the morning, as usual.

  • He makes the house louder and happier.

  • Four legs, big heart.

  • Best listener, worst alarm clock.

Making them your own

Name your pet and give them a voice. It makes the post feel alive.

Each button copies the line exactly. The WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp with the line ready to send to a chat or to set as a status.

Questions people ask

Can I change these captions?

Yes. Change a word or add a detail of your own, such as a place or a name, so that the line fits your photo.

Are these captions free to use?

Yes, for your own posts and status. Please do not copy the whole page to another site.
