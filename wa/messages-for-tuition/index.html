WhatsApp messages for a tuition and coaching

First messages your customers can send you, ready to use in a click-to-chat link.

Parents usually want the same four facts: class, timing, fee and whether a demo is possible. A pre-filled message lets them ask in the way that gets you the details you need.

Tap "Make a link" under a message to open the link maker with that text in the box, then add your number. Fill the blanks (____) with your own details first if you want the message to be exact.

  • Hello, I want to know about tuition for class ____ (subject ____). What are the timings?

    Make a link

  • Hi, can my child attend a demo class? Class ____.

    Make a link

  • Hello, please share the fee details and batch timings.

    Make a link

  • Hi, I am interested in online classes for ____. Do you have a batch in the evening?

    Make a link

  • Hello, my child needs help with ____. Can you suggest a plan?

    Make a link

  • Hi, do you have a seat in the new batch starting next month?

    Make a link

A tip for this kind of business

Keep your reply ready with batch details and fee in one message, so you do not type it fifty times.

Keep the first reply ready too. When a customer writes, answer with what you offer, a price or a time, and the next step. See also how pre-filled messages work and privacy and safety.

Questions people ask

Do customers have to send this exact message?

No. The message appears in their text box and they can change it before sending. It is only a helpful start.

Can I put my own words in?

Yes. Replace the blanks (____) and edit any line. Then use the link maker to turn it into a link or QR code.
