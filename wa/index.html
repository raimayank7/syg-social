How to use it

  1. Choose your country and type the number the way you dial it locally. Leave out the 0 at the start.
  2. Optionally type the first message you want customers to send.
  3. Copy the link or download the QR code.
  4. Tap "Test the link" on your phone to check that it opens the right chat.

The tool follows WhatsApp's own click-to-chat format. It does not send anything: a person who taps the link still has to press send. Nothing you type leaves your browser. Read how click to chat works, then the guides below.
