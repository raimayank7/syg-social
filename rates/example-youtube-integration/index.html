Worked example: mention inside a youtube video

How the price is built, step by step, with made-up numbers.

Made-up example. The hours and the hourly value below are invented to show the method. They are not market rates and not advice on what to charge. Use your own numbers.

A teacher mentions a stationery brand for 30 seconds in a lesson video. The brand asks that she mention no rival for a month.

The numbers in this example

ItemValue
Pieces1
Hours for one piece1 planning, 0.5 shooting, 1 editing, 0.5 revisions = 3.0 hours
Hourly value (made up)Rs 500
Costs paid by the creatorRs 0
Add-onsexclusivity 25%, tax and fees set aside 10%

The working

  1. Time for one piece: 3.0 hours x Rs 500 = Rs 1,500.
  2. Time for 1 piece(s): Rs 1,500.
  3. Plus costs of Rs 0: subtotal Rs 1,500.
  4. With add-ons the price before tax and fees is Rs 1,875.
  5. Tax and fees: the price is raised so that the creator keeps the amount after setting aside 10% of the final price, giving Rs 2,083.

Quote: Rs 2,083 (Rs 2,083 per piece)

Open this in the calculator and change the numbers

What to learn from it

The price is a sum of parts you can explain. If the brand wants less, remove a part (the rush, the exclusivity, a round of changes) instead of cutting your hourly value. Read how to price a collaboration, usage rights and negotiating.
