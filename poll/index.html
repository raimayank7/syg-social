Make a group poll in a minute

Type a question, share the link, and watch the votes come in. No login, no app.

How it works

Write a question and two to eight options, choose how long the poll stays open, and press Make my poll. You get a link to share in WhatsApp or anywhere else, and a private admin link that lets you close the poll early. Each device can vote once, and nobody has to give a name.

Polls here are informal guides for friendly decisions, not official counts. Read how polls work, how to run a fair poll and the limits of anonymous voting. This site is independent.
