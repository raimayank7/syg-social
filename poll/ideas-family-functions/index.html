Poll ideas: family functions

Planning a wedding, birthday or festival with relatives.

Family groups are big and opinions are many. A poll lets everyone speak without a long chain of replies. Offer clear options and a closing date, and accept the result with good grace.

Say in the group when the poll closes, so that nobody feels left out.

Before you share

Check the options fit your group, add the end date, and say in your message what the poll is for. One poll should settle one thing. See how to run a fair poll and polls in WhatsApp groups.

Questions people ask

How do I use one of these polls?

Press "Use this poll". The home page opens with the question and options filled in. Change anything you like, choose the number of days and press Make my poll.

Can the group see who voted?

No. Names are never asked or stored. Read the limits of anonymous voting.
