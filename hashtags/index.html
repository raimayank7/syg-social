How to use the lists

Open the list that matches your post and tap the tags you want. Each list has broad tags that place your post in a subject, specific tags that real people search, and, where it helps, Hindi or Hinglish tags. Use a few that truly fit, and add one tag that is your own.

Not sure where to start? Try the hashtag builder, or read how to choose hashtags and how many to use. For businesses there is a page on brand, local and campaign tags.
