Ganesh Chaturthi and Janmashtami hashtags

Tags for Ganpati festivals and Krishna Janmashtami.

Festival tags for these celebrations are devotional. Use the name of the festival and what you show: the idol, the decoration, the dahi handi. Keep the tone respectful.

Use eco-friendly materials in your own celebration if you can, and say so.

Ganesh

Janmashtami

Mood

Post ideas for this festival

  • A photo of the idol and decorations made at home.
  • A modak recipe post.
  • A post about an eco-friendly celebration.
  • A picture of the community pandal, with permission.
  • A thank-you to those who helped.

Post a few days before as well as on the day, because people look for ideas ahead of time.

Using these tags

Tap a tag to copy it, or "Copy all" under a group to copy that group in one go. Paste the tags after your caption, then remove any that do not fit the post. The builder can mix this list with a festival and your own city. Read how to choose hashtags if you are new to this.

Questions people ask

Will these hashtags make my post go viral?

No tag can promise that. They help the right people find a post. Good content, a clear caption and regular posting matter more.

Can I use all the tags on this page?

You can, but it is better to pick the few that describe your post. Delete any that do not fit.
