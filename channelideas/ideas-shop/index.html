Channel ideas for shop and local business

Offers, new stock and opening hours in one place.

A shop channel works like a notice board that customers can choose to follow. Post only when you have something useful: new stock, a festival timing, a short offer. Followers can react or vote on a poll, but they cannot message you through the channel.

Put the link or QR code of your channel on the bill and the shop door.

  • New arrival

    A photo and one line with the name and price.

  • Festival timings

    Shop hours for the festival days.

  • Offer of the week

    One offer, with the end date.

  • Back in stock

    Tell followers when a popular item returns.

  • How it is made

    A short video of the making or packing.

  • Customer question

    Answer one question that many people ask.

  • Poll: what next

    Ask which product you should stock next.

  • Closed notice

    Post at once when you are closed unexpectedly.

  • Care tip

    How to look after the product they bought.

  • Behind the counter

    A photo of your team, with permission.

  • Local news

    A useful local note: road closed, market day.

  • Thank you

    Thank followers for a milestone.

Making it your own

Pick five ideas, write them in your own words with your own details and photos, and post them over two weeks. Then look at the reactions and poll votes to see what your followers like. Read how channels work and post formats first if you are new.

Questions people ask

Can followers reply to these updates?

Not directly. WhatsApp's help page says channel updates are a one-way broadcast, and followers can react or vote on polls. Use a poll when you want an answer.

How often should I post?

As often as you can keep useful, often two to four times a week. See the posting plan.
