Channel ideas for school, class and tuition

A one-way channel for notices, homework and results.

A channel suits school news because parents want to read, not reply. Updates stay in a separate tab and do not mix with family chats. Keep each update short and dated.

Never post a child's marks, photo or private details on a public channel; use a private chat for those.

  • Homework of the day

    One update each evening with the subjects and the date.

  • Test calendar

    A weekly update listing the tests of the coming week.

  • Holiday notice

    Post the date and the reopening date the day the school decides.

  • Fee date reminder

    A short reminder three days before the due date.

  • Syllabus for the unit

    Post the list of topics, then an update when each is covered.

  • Question of the week

    A poll with a revision question; followers vote.

  • Study tip

    One method each week: how to revise, how to plan.

  • Event photos

    Photos of events with the school's permission, no names of children.

  • Result day note

    Post when and how to see results; no marks in the channel.

  • Meeting dates

    Parent-teacher meeting date, time and place.

  • Library or book of the month

    Name one book and say why.

  • Thank-you note

    Thank volunteers and helpers by role.

Making it your own

Pick five ideas, write them in your own words with your own details and photos, and post them over two weeks. Then look at the reactions and poll votes to see what your followers like. Read how channels work and post formats first if you are new.

Questions people ask

Can followers reply to these updates?

Not directly. WhatsApp's help page says channel updates are a one-way broadcast, and followers can react or vote on polls. Use a poll when you want an answer.

How often should I post?

As often as you can keep useful, often two to four times a week. See the posting plan.
