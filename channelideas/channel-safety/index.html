Channel safety and privacy for admins

What admins should do to keep a channel safe: content care, follower privacy, not sharing private details and what to do about abuse.

WhatsApp's help page about channels explains that followers' privacy is protected: people viewing a channel cannot see who follows it, and admins can see a follower's profile name and sometimes their photo next to reactions and votes. As an admin you hold power over what is shown, so take it seriously.

Habits for admins

  • Post only what you have the right to share. Credit photos and text that are not yours.
  • Do not post private details of other people, including children.
  • Do not share rumours. Check a fact before you post it and say where it came from.
  • Keep content suitable for all ages unless the channel says otherwise.
  • Do not use what you see about followers (their names on votes) for anything but running the channel.

Protect your own number

Read WhatsApp's pages on safety and privacy for admins and on channel admin controls, and use a business number where you can; our note on business or personal numbers helps.

If someone causes trouble

Followers cannot message admins through the channel, which limits abuse. If you receive harassment elsewhere, block and report inside the app and keep a record.

Channel facts on this page were read on 14 November 2026 on WhatsApp's help pages About WhatsApp Channels and How to create a WhatsApp Channel. WhatsApp changes the app often, so check the app for your own options.
