Food and drink emoji

Fruit, vegetables, meals, sweets and drinks.

Food emoji make a menu, a shopping list or a dinner plan more fun, and they are heavily used by cooks, cafes and shops. The set covers many world foods, though far from all Indian dishes, so check the list of Indian-food emoji for the closest picture to each.

Pictures are small, so people use a food emoji as a symbol rather than an exact picture: a bowl for any curry, a flatbread for any roti. If you are selling food, put the exact item name in text beside the emoji so customers are not confused.

Using these emoji

Tap an emoji to copy it, then paste it where you want it. Names come from Unicode and are what a screen reader says aloud. Read emoji and accessibility for how to keep messages easy to hear, and how to type emoji if you want to use your keyboard instead.

Questions people ask

How do I copy an emoji?

Tap or click the emoji. It is copied, and you can paste it into any app.

Why does an emoji look different on my friend's phone?

Each company draws its own pictures for the same emoji. The meaning stays the same, the style differs.
