People and body emoji

Hands, gestures, people, jobs and families.

This group holds hand gestures, body parts, people in many jobs and activities, and families. Many of them can be shown in different skin tones; the lists here show the default form, and the skin tones guide explains how to change it.

Hand gestures are the part that needs the most care. A gesture that means "good" in one country may be rude in another, and the same hand can mean thanks, prayer or a plea. When you write to people from other places, choose gestures you know are safe, and read our gesture guide.

Using these emoji

Tap an emoji to copy it, then paste it where you want it. Names come from Unicode and are what a screen reader says aloud. Read emoji and accessibility for how to keep messages easy to hear, and how to type emoji if you want to use your keyboard instead.

Questions people ask

How do I copy an emoji?

Tap or click the emoji. It is copied, and you can paste it into any app.

Why does an emoji look different on my friend's phone?

Each company draws its own pictures for the same emoji. The meaning stays the same, the style differs.
