Smileys and emotion emoji

Faces, hearts and feelings: the emoji people use most.

These are the faces and hearts that carry most of our feelings in text. A face emoji is a stand-in for the tone of voice and the expression we lose when we type, which is why one small picture can turn a plain sentence into a joke, a thank-you or a worry.

Meaning depends on who sends it and who reads it. The same face can mean different things to a teenager and to a grandparent, and apps draw each emoji a little differently. Use the names below as a guide, read the sentence around the emoji and, when the message matters, say the feeling in words as well.

Using these emoji

Tap an emoji to copy it, then paste it where you want it. Names come from Unicode and are what a screen reader says aloud. Read emoji and accessibility for how to keep messages easy to hear, and how to type emoji if you want to use your keyboard instead.

Questions people ask

How do I copy an emoji?

Tap or click the emoji. It is copied, and you can paste it into any app.

Why does an emoji look different on my friend's phone?

Each company draws its own pictures for the same emoji. The meaning stays the same, the style differs.
