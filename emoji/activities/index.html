Activity emoji

Events, sports, games and arts and crafts.

Parties, medals, sports and games live here. They are used to cheer on a team, to invite friends to an event or to describe a hobby. A trophy or a ball says what you did without a sentence.

Many sport emoji look alike at small sizes. If you are organising a match, put the sport, the time and the place in writing, and let the emoji add colour.

Using these emoji

Tap an emoji to copy it, then paste it where you want it. Names come from Unicode and are what a screen reader says aloud. Read emoji and accessibility for how to keep messages easy to hear, and how to type emoji if you want to use your keyboard instead.

Questions people ask

How do I copy an emoji?

Tap or click the emoji. It is copied, and you can paste it into any app.

Why does an emoji look different on my friend's phone?

Each company draws its own pictures for the same emoji. The meaning stays the same, the style differs.
