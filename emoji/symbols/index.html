Symbol emoji

Signs, arrows, warnings, shapes, keycaps and religious symbols.

Symbols include the heart shapes that are not faces, arrows and signs, warning triangles, religious symbols, zodiac signs, coloured circles, letters and keycap numbers. They are the useful grammar of a message: they point, warn, number and separate.

Religious symbols carry deep meaning for people. Use them with respect, in the right context, and never as decoration that could look careless to a believer.

Using these emoji

Tap an emoji to copy it, then paste it where you want it. Names come from Unicode and are what a screen reader says aloud. Read emoji and accessibility for how to keep messages easy to hear, and how to type emoji if you want to use your keyboard instead.

Questions people ask

How do I copy an emoji?

Tap or click the emoji. It is copied, and you can paste it into any app.

Why does an emoji look different on my friend's phone?

Each company draws its own pictures for the same emoji. The meaning stays the same, the style differs.
