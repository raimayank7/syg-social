How it works

Open the group of messages that fits your situation, pick one, and fill the blanks (____) with your own details. Tap "Send on WhatsApp" to open WhatsApp with the message ready, then choose who receives it. Nothing is sent until you press send.

Read how to write short business messages, and how to remind someone to pay politely. If customers should be able to message you with one tap, make a link or QR code with the WhatsApp link maker.
