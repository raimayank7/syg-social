Keeping business WhatsApp chats as records

How to keep important customer messages safe: why records matter, what to save, how to back up chats and how to avoid losing an order or a promise.

Many small businesses take orders and agree prices on WhatsApp. These chats are records. If a customer says "you promised Rs 500" or "I paid on Monday", the chat can settle it. Treat the important ones with care.

What to keep

  • Orders and quotes with the price and date.
  • Payment confirmations and screenshots.
  • Delivery and warranty promises.
  • Complaints and how you resolved them.

How to keep them safe

  1. Back up your chats. Use the app's own backup option so that you do not lose them if you change or lose your phone. Read WhatsApp's help page for the steps on your phone.
  2. Write a summary. For a big order, write the key details in a note or a sheet with the date, so you do not have to scroll.
  3. Star important messages so they are easy to find.
  4. Use a separate business number so that business messages are not mixed with family chats.

Respect privacy

Keep customers' details private. Do not share their messages or photos with others without their permission. Do not store more than you need.

When a dispute starts

Reply calmly, quote the original message and offer a fair solution. If it grows, show the record to a mediator or the consumer authority. For polite wording see apology and delay messages.

Questions people ask

Can a WhatsApp chat be used as proof?

It can be used as evidence of what was said, but how it is treated depends on the case and the law. If a matter is serious, ask a lawyer.
