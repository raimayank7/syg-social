Video ideas for home cooking and food

Ideas for a home cook, a tiffin service or a street stall.

Food is the most watched subject on short video, so the way to stand out is to be specific and honest: one dish, one trick, one real kitchen. These ideas are shaped for people who cook for family or customers.

Film in daylight near a window. Steam and sizzle sound do half the work, so keep the pan noise.

  • One-pan lunch in under a minute

    Film from above, three or four fast cuts, finish with the plate.

  • The ingredient list on one plate

    Lay everything out, point, then cook quickly.

  • Mistake that ruins your roti

    Show the wrong way first, then the right way.

  • Leftovers into a new dish

    Open the fridge, pick two things, show the result.

  • How my mother makes it

    Ask her to talk while you film her hands.

  • Three chutneys, one thali

    Fast shots of each chutney, then the full plate.

  • Tiffin packing for the week

    Film the Sunday prep in time-lapse.

  • Street food stall behind the scenes

    Ask the owner first, then film the setup at dawn.

  • A recipe with only four ingredients

    Show each ingredient on screen with its name.

  • Taste test with a family member

    Give a real, short reaction, no acting.

  • The pan I use and why

    Hold it up, show one close-up of the cooking.

  • Order packing with care

    Film the final box, the label and a handwritten note.

Making them work

Pick one idea, write a hook line and the three parts of the video (see reel structure), then film it in one go in good light. Post it with a clear caption, and check how it did after a few days. Keep an idea list so that you never start the week with a blank page.

Questions people ask

Do I need to follow the idea exactly?

No. Use it as a starting point and put your own place, product and voice into it. The same idea looks different when you do it.

How do I start a video?

Open with a hook. See our question hooks and promise hooks.
