How to use this site

Tap "Give me an idea" for a random idea and a hook, or open the list for your niche. Every idea says how to film it in a sentence, so you can start the same day with a phone.

New to short video? Read how to film with only a phone, then reel structure and a weekly plan. Check music rules before you pick a song.
