Video ideas for salon, beauty and grooming

Ideas for salons, makeup artists and beauty creators.

Beauty videos need good light and honesty. Show real work, with permission from clients, and avoid claims that a product will cure a skin problem. Ideas below focus on process.

Film near a window. If you use a ring light, check that it does not make skin look flat.

  • Quick everyday makeup

    Film from the front, name each product.

  • Hair care routine for monsoon

    Show steps and say what you use.

  • Bridal trial, before and after

    With client permission, same angle.

  • Three hairstyles in a minute

    One take with quick cuts.

  • Mehendi design time-lapse

    Film the hand from above.

  • Nail art in a few steps

    Close-up, steady camera.

  • Skin routine for a beginner

    Show order of steps and say it is general advice.

  • How I clean my brushes

    Film in the sink, calm voice.

  • A day at the salon

    Time-lapse of opening and setup.

  • Haircut myths

    Say one myth and correct it.

  • Matching a lipstick to an outfit

    Show three tries.

  • Self-care Sunday

    A slow routine with calm sound.

Making them work

Pick one idea, write a hook line and the three parts of the video (see reel structure), then film it in one go in good light. Post it with a clear caption, and check how it did after a few days. Keep an idea list so that you never start the week with a blank page.

Questions people ask

Do I need to follow the idea exactly?

No. Use it as a starting point and put your own place, product and voice into it. The same idea looks different when you do it.

How do I start a video?

Open with a hook. See our question hooks and promise hooks.
