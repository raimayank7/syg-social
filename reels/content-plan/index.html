A weekly content plan for short videos

How to plan, film and post a week of short videos in one sitting: a simple routine with a table, so that posting stays regular without daily stress.

The hardest part of posting is not filming; it is deciding what to film each day. A weekly plan removes that decision. Choose a day to plan and film for the whole week, and your posting stays regular.

A simple weekly plan

DayType of videoExample for a home baker
MondayTeach one tipHow to keep a cake moist
WednesdayBehind the scenesPacking today's orders
FridayCustomer story or questionAnswer a question from the week
SundayLight and personalA quiet bake for the family

How to batch

  1. Plan three or four ideas. Use the idea picker or a niche list.
  2. Write the hook for each, one line.
  3. Film all of them in one sitting, in the same place with the same light.
  4. Edit and schedule them, or post them on the days you chose.

Keep it small

Three videos a week that you can keep up are better than daily videos you quit after a fortnight. Start small and add one more only when the routine is easy.

Review once a month

Look at which videos people watched to the end, saved or sent to friends. Make more like them. Read common mistakes if a video does not do well.

Questions people ask

How many videos a week should I post?

As many as you can keep up. Regular posting matters more than frequency. Three a week is a good place to start for most small accounts.
