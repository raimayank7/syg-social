Question hooks

Opening questions that make people stay.

A question puts the viewer inside the video: they want to know the answer. The best ones are narrow and real, not "Do you want to be rich?". Fill the blanks with your own subject.

Replace each ____ with your own subject. Say the line in the first seconds, then give the answer in the rest of the video.

  • Do you make this mistake when you ____?

  • Why does ____ always go wrong?

  • What if you could ____ in under a minute?

  • Have you ever wondered why ____?

  • Do you know what to do when ____?

  • Which is better for ____: A or B?

  • Did you know that ____ is easier than it looks?

  • Why does nobody tell you this about ____?

  • Can you spot the difference between ____ and ____?

  • What would you do if ____?

  • How do I ____ without spending much?

  • Is it true that ____?

  • What is the first thing to check when ____?

  • Why is my ____ not working?

  • Have you tried this simple trick for ____?

  • Who else does this when they ____?

  • Do you really need ____?

  • What do I wish I knew before ____?

  • How long does it take to ____?

  • Are you still doing ____ the hard way?
