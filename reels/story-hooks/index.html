Story hooks

Openings that start a small story.

People follow a story to its end. Open in the middle of a moment, with a place and a feeling, and keep it true. A short story hook turns a how-to into something people remember.

Replace each ____ with your own subject. Say the line in the first seconds, then give the answer in the rest of the video.

  • Last week I almost ____, and then ____.

  • Yesterday something small happened at the ____.

  • I used to think ____. I was wrong.

  • My mother told me ____, and she was right.

  • Three years ago I ____. This is what changed.

  • It started with a ____ and ended with ____.

  • A customer asked me ____, and I had no answer.

  • I made this mistake on my first ____.

  • Today I finally ____, after months of trying.

  • This is a picture of the day I ____.

  • I did not believe ____ until I tried it.

  • Nobody was watching when I ____.

  • At the market this morning, I saw ____.

  • My teacher once said ____.

  • I got a message from ____ that changed my day.

  • Here is what I learned from ____ in a week.

  • I failed at ____ twice before this worked.

  • Let me tell you about the time I ____.

  • My first day at ____ looked like this.

  • The best advice I got was ____.
