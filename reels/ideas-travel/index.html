Video ideas for travel and local trips

Ideas for travellers and local explorers.

Travel videos are most useful when they help someone else go. Share costs, timings, mistakes and small discoveries, not just views. Short and honest wins.

Show the place name on screen early. People search for the name.

  • A hidden spot near my city

    Walk in and show the entry, timings and cost.

  • Packing for a three-day trip

    Lay out clothes and pack in a quick cut.

  • Train or bus: what I chose

    Compare time and cost on screen.

  • What I spent on a weekend

    Show a simple list on screen.

  • A food I tried only here

    Film the stall and the first bite.

  • Mistake I made on this trip

    Say it plainly, add the lesson.

  • Sunrise at the viewpoint

    One long calm shot with sound.

  • Local transport tips

    Show where to buy tickets.

  • Three things to carry in the hills

    Hold each item up.

  • A small town in 60 seconds

    Fast cuts of streets, shops and food.

  • Photo spot, how to get there

    Show the route from the main road.

  • A day with no plan

    Film what happens.

Making them work

Pick one idea, write a hook line and the three parts of the video (see reel structure), then film it in one go in good light. Post it with a clear caption, and check how it did after a few days. Keep an idea list so that you never start the week with a blank page.

Questions people ask

Do I need to follow the idea exactly?

No. Use it as a starting point and put your own place, product and voice into it. The same idea looks different when you do it.

How do I start a video?

Open with a hook. See our question hooks and promise hooks.
