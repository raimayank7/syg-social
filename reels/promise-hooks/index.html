Promise hooks

Openings that tell the viewer what they will get.

A promise hook says what the viewer gets and how long it takes. Only promise what the video delivers: a broken promise loses trust. Keep the promise small and true.

Replace each ____ with your own subject. Say the line in the first seconds, then give the answer in the rest of the video.

  • In 30 seconds I will show you how to ____.

  • Here is one simple way to ____.

  • By the end of this video you will know ____.

  • Three steps to ____, no tools needed.

  • I am going to show you the easiest way to ____.

  • This one trick saves me time with ____.

  • Here is exactly what I do to ____.

  • A quick fix for ____.

  • Let me show you how I ____ at home.

  • Five minutes is enough to ____.

  • Here is the simple method I use for ____.

  • This is the shortcut I wish I had earlier for ____.

  • Copy this and you can ____ today.

  • You do not need much to ____. Watch.

  • Let me save you an hour with ____.

  • The easiest recipe, tip or hack for ____ is coming up.

  • Do this before you ____.

  • One checklist for ____. Save it.

  • The right way to ____ in plain steps.

  • Stay for the last tip about ____.
