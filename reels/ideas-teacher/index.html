Video ideas for teachers and tutors

Ideas for teachers, coaches and tutors who want to share a little knowledge.

A good teaching video answers one question in under a minute. It does not try to teach a whole chapter. These ideas give you a repeatable series that students can follow.

Write the question on a sheet of paper and hold it up first. It tells viewers in a second what they will learn.

  • One formula in one minute

    Write it large on a board and explain once.

  • Common mistake in class 10 maths

    Show the wrong step and the right one.

  • Word of the day with a sentence

    Show the word, say the meaning, use it twice.

  • A science experiment from the kitchen

    Film each step slowly and ask a question at the end.

  • Memory trick for a list

    Say the trick and show it on a sheet.

  • How to read a question paper

    Film the paper and mark key words.

  • Study timetable for a busy week

    Draw a table and fill it live.

  • Grammar in 30 seconds

    One rule, two examples.

  • Ask me a doubt

    Read a student doubt and answer.

  • Map skills in three steps

    Draw a quick map and mark.

  • Parent question: how much should my child study

    Give a calm and honest answer.

  • My favourite book for beginners

    Hold it up and say why, in a few lines.

Making them work

Pick one idea, write a hook line and the three parts of the video (see reel structure), then film it in one go in good light. Post it with a clear caption, and check how it did after a few days. Keep an idea list so that you never start the week with a blank page.

Questions people ask

Do I need to follow the idea exactly?

No. Use it as a starting point and put your own place, product and voice into it. The same idea looks different when you do it.

How do I start a video?

Open with a hook. See our question hooks and promise hooks.
