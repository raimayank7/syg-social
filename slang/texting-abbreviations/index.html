Chat Slang

Texting abbreviations

The short forms people type in chat every day.

Texting abbreviations save thumbs. Most are older than smartphones, and the common ones are understood in every country where English is used in chat. This page explains the ones you will meet most often and shows how each is used in a sentence.

Use them with friends and colleagues who use them too. In a formal email or a message to someone older, write the full words.

  • BRB

    Be right back; you are stepping away for a short while.

    Example: "brb, answering the door"

  • OMW

    On my way; you have left and are coming.

    Example: "omw, ten minutes"

  • IDK

    I do not know.

    Example: "idk what to cook tonight"

  • BTW

    By the way; you are adding a side thought.

    Example: "btw, the meeting moved to four"

  • FYI

    For your information; a note that needs no reply.

    Example: "fyi, the office is closed on Friday"

  • TBH

    To be honest; you are about to say what you really think.

    Example: "tbh the film was too long"

  • IMO

    In my opinion; a softer way to give a view.

    Example: "imo the blue one looks better"

  • ASAP

    As soon as possible; used for something urgent.

    Example: "send the file asap please"

  • TTYL

    Talk to you later; a goodbye in chat.

    Example: "ttyl, dinner is ready"

  • NP

    No problem; a reply to thanks.

    Example: "thanks for the lift!" "np"

  • THX

    Thanks.

    Example: "thx for the help"

  • RN

    Right now.

    Example: "I am in a meeting rn"

  • IRL

    In real life; as opposed to online.

    Example: "we have never met irl"

  • DM

    Direct message; a private message on a social app.

    Example: "dm me the details"

  • ETA

    Estimated time of arrival; when you expect to get there.

    Example: "what is your eta?"

  • TMI

    Too much information; said when someone shares more than you wanted.

    Example: "okay, that is tmi"

  • LOL

    Laughing out loud; in practice it often just means "that is funny".

    Example: "lol that photo is great"

  • NVM

    Never mind; forget what I asked.

    Example: "nvm, found it"

  • AFAIK

    As far as I know; you are not sure.

    Example: "afaik the shop opens at nine"

  • GM / GN

    Good morning; good night.

    Example: "gm everyone"

Questions people ask

Do these words mean the same everywhere?

Mostly, but not always. Slang changes with the group, the app and the year. Use the meaning here as the usual one and check with the context.

Why is a word not listed?

We list words that are common and not mainly abusive. Tell us on the contact page if you want one added.
