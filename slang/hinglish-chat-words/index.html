Hinglish chat words

Hindi words that appear in English chats across India.

Hinglish chat is half the way India types today. These words are not slang in the strict sense; they are everyday Hindi that slips into English sentences. The list shows the usual Roman spelling, the meaning and a sample line.

Spellings vary, so do not worry about the exact letters. Use the words with people who share your language, and explain them to others.

  • Yaar

    Friend, buddy; also used to add warmth or frustration.

    Example: "yaar, come fast"

  • Bhai

    Brother; used for male friends and often for anyone.

    Example: "bhai, I will call you"

  • Scene

    Situation or plan.

    Example: "what is the scene tonight?"

  • Timepass

    Passing time without a purpose.

    Example: "just timepass, nothing serious"

  • Jugaad

    A clever, cheap fix made with what you have.

    Example: "I did a jugaad with a clip"

  • Bindaas

    Carefree, cool.

    Example: "she is bindaas about everything"

  • Paka mat

    Do not bore me.

    Example: "paka mat yaar, say it short"

  • Funda

    Idea or principle (from "fundamental").

    Example: "what is your funda?"

  • Chill maar

    Relax.

    Example: "chill maar, we have time"

  • Lafda

    A hassle or a fight.

    Example: "I do not want any lafda"

  • Set hai

    It is arranged, all fine.

    Example: "plan set hai"

  • Bakwaas

    Nonsense.

    Example: "this is total bakwaas"

  • Mast

    Great, excellent.

    Example: "the food was mast"

  • Pakka

    Sure, definite.

    Example: "pakka, I will come"

  • Chalo

    Let us go; also "okay then".

    Example: "chalo, see you tomorrow"

  • Arre

    An exclamation of surprise or a way to get attention.

    Example: "arre, look at that"

  • Theek hai

    Okay, fine.

    Example: "theek hai, send it"

  • Jaldi

    Quickly.

    Example: "jaldi aao"

  • Dhoka

    Betrayal, being cheated.

    Example: "the shop gave me dhoka"

  • Sahi hai

    That is right, good.

    Example: "sahi hai, well done"

Questions people ask

Do these words mean the same everywhere?

Mostly, but not always. Slang changes with the group, the app and the year. Use the meaning here as the usual one and check with the context.

Why is a word not listed?

We list words that are common and not mainly abusive. Tell us on the contact page if you want one added.
