Dating and relationship slang

Words for the early and messy stages of relationships.

People invent new words for relationship habits because the old words did not fit. These terms are used with a sense of humour and sometimes with some pain. Understanding them helps you read what friends are saying and talk about your own situation.

These are descriptions of behaviour, not insults. Use them to talk about what you want, not to label a person.

  • Situationship

    A relationship with no clear label or commitment.

    Example: "we are in a situationship"

  • Ghosting

    Cutting off contact without any explanation.

    Example: "he ghosted me for a month"

  • Breadcrumbing

    Giving small bits of attention to keep someone interested without commitment.

    Example: "she is breadcrumbing me"

  • Red flag

    A warning sign about a person's behaviour.

    Example: "never apologising is a red flag"

  • Green flag

    A good sign about a person's behaviour.

    Example: "he listens, which is a green flag"

  • Catfish

    Someone who pretends to be a different person online.

    Example: "the profile was a catfish"

  • Love bombing

    Overwhelming someone with attention to gain control.

    Example: "it felt like love bombing"

  • DTR

    Define the relationship; the talk about what you are to each other.

    Example: "we need a dtr talk"

  • Soft launch

    Hinting at a new partner in a post without showing them.

    Example: "she soft launched him with a hand photo"

  • Hard launch

    Openly showing a partner for the first time.

    Example: "the hard launch is today"

  • Talking stage

    The early period of getting to know someone.

    Example: "we are in the talking stage"

  • Simp

    Someone who does too much for a person who does not return it.

    Example: "do not simp for him"

  • Rizz

    Charm in attracting someone.

    Example: "he is all rizz"

  • Left on read

    Seeing a message and not replying.

    Example: "left on read again"

  • Benching

    Keeping someone as a backup while dating others.

    Example: "I felt benched"

  • Slow fade

    Gradually reducing contact.

    Example: "it was a slow fade"

  • Friend zone

    Being seen only as a friend by someone you like.

    Example: "he put me in the friend zone"

  • Ex

    A former partner.

    Example: "I ran into my ex"

  • Rebound

    A relationship soon after a breakup.

    Example: "he is a rebound"

  • Ick

    A sudden loss of attraction because of a small thing.

    Example: "that gave me the ick"

Questions people ask

Do these words mean the same everywhere?

Mostly, but not always. Slang changes with the group, the app and the year. Use the meaning here as the usual one and check with the context.

Why is a word not listed?

We list words that are common and not mainly abusive. Tell us on the contact page if you want one added.
