Social media abbreviations

Short forms you see in comments, captions and group chats.

Social media has its own shorthand. Some of these began on message boards and some on apps, and many now appear in captions and comments. Knowing them helps you read a thread without feeling lost.

If you are not sure of a short form, look it up before you reply. A wrong guess can make a reply sound odd or rude.

  • SMH

    Shaking my head; mild disbelief or disappointment.

    Example: "he forgot his own birthday, smh"

  • FOMO

    Fear of missing out; worry that others are doing something fun without you.

    Example: "I joined the trip out of fomo"

  • JOMO

    Joy of missing out; being glad you skipped something.

    Example: "staying home with tea. jomo"

  • TBT

    Throwback Thursday; an old photo posted on a Thursday.

    Example: "tbt to our school trip"

  • OOTD

    Outfit of the day; a photo of what you are wearing.

    Example: "ootd for the wedding"

  • POV

    Point of view; used to set up a short scene in a video or caption.

    Example: "pov: you found the last samosa"

  • FTW

    For the win; a cheer for something good.

    Example: "chai ftw"

  • ICYMI

    In case you missed it; used when posting something again.

    Example: "icymi: our shop is open on Sunday"

  • AMA

    Ask me anything; an invitation for questions.

    Example: "I am a baker, ama"

  • TL;DR

    Too long, did not read; a short summary of a long post.

    Example: "tl;dr: the train is delayed"

  • OP

    Original poster; the person who started the thread.

    Example: "the op never replied"

  • FAQ

    Frequently asked questions.

    Example: "check the faq first"

  • BFF

    Best friends forever.

    Example: "my bff came over"

  • GOAT

    Greatest of all time; high praise for a person.

    Example: "that chef is the goat"

  • NSFW

    Not safe for work; warns that content is not suitable at a workplace.

    Example: "mark it nsfw before posting"

  • IMHO

    In my humble opinion; a polite way to share a view.

    Example: "imho the first plan was better"

  • YOLO

    You only live once; an excuse to try something.

    Example: "yolo, I booked the trip"

  • HMU

    Hit me up; contact me.

    Example: "hmu when you reach"

  • LMK

    Let me know.

    Example: "lmk if you can come"

  • RT

    Retweet or repost; sharing someone else's post.

    Example: "rt if you agree"

Questions people ask

Do these words mean the same everywhere?

Mostly, but not always. Slang changes with the group, the app and the year. Use the meaning here as the usual one and check with the context.

Why is a word not listed?

We list words that are common and not mainly abusive. Tell us on the contact page if you want one added.
