Chat Slang

A to Z of slang

Every word on this site in one list, with its short meaning. Tap "more" for the page with an example.

This index gathers all 240 words from the lists on this site in alphabetical order. Use the search box to find a word fast, or jump by letter. Short forms such as BRB or TTYL are listed under their first letter. Where two lists use the same word with a slightly different sense, you will see it twice, once for each list.

A

  • Aaj kal: these days more
  • Aaj nahi: not today more
  • Ace: to do very well at something more
  • Admin: a person who manages a group and can add or remove members more
  • AFAIK: as far as I know; you are not sure more
  • AFK: away from keyboard; the player has left for a while more
  • Algorithm: the rules an app uses to decide what you see more
  • Algorithm: the app's rules for what to show to whom more
  • AMA: ask me anything; an invitation for questions more
  • Analytics: the numbers an app shows about your posts more
  • Archive: to hide a chat without deleting it more
  • Arre: an exclamation of surprise or a way to get attention more
  • ASAP: as soon as possible; used for something urgent more
  • Awesome: very good more

B

  • B-roll: extra footage that covers the main video more
  • Bakwaas: nonsense more
  • Bandwidth: how much work a person can take on more
  • Bas yahin: just here, nearby more
  • Batch: to make several posts in one session more
  • Benching: keeping someone as a backup while dating others more
  • Bet: okay, deal; also used as "yes, I agree" more
  • BFF: best friends forever more
  • Bhai: brother; used for male friends and often for anyone more
  • Bindaas: carefree, cool more
  • Binge: to do something a lot in one go, such as watching many episodes more
  • Bore ho raha hoon: I am bored more
  • Boss: a strong enemy at the end of a level more
  • BRB: be right back; you are stepping away for a short while more
  • Breadcrumbing: giving small bits of attention to keep someone interested without commitment more
  • Broadcast: a message sent to many people at once without a group more
  • Broke: having no money for now more
  • BTW: by the way; you are adding a side thought more
  • Buff: to make something stronger in an update more
  • Bummer: a disappointing thing more
  • Burnout: exhaustion from long stress at work more
  • Buzz: a feeling of excitement, or to call someone more

C

  • Camp: to wait in one spot for opponents more
  • Caption: the text with a post more
  • Catch up: to meet and talk after some time more
  • Catfish: someone who pretends to be a different person online more
  • Chal raha hai: it is going on, so-so more
  • Chalo: let us go; also "okay then" more
  • Cheesy: too sentimental or corny more
  • Chill: to relax, or calm more
  • Chill maar: relax more
  • Circle back: return to a topic later more
  • Clapback: a sharp reply to criticism more
  • Clickbait: a title made to make you click, often misleading more
  • Clutch: winning in a tough moment more
  • Co-op: playing together as a team more
  • Collab: a post made together with another creator or brand more
  • Community: a space that holds several related groups more
  • Cooked: in serious trouble, or doomed more
  • Cool: good, fine, impressive more
  • Crash: to fall asleep, or to stay overnight more
  • Cringe: something that makes you feel embarrassed more
  • CTA: call to action; what you ask the viewer to do next more

D

  • Deadline: the time by which work must be done more
  • Dekhte hain: we will see; often a polite maybe more
  • Delulu: delusional; having unrealistic hopes, said with humour more
  • Dhoka: betrayal, being cheated more
  • Dil khush ho gaya: that made me very happy more
  • Disappearing messages: messages that delete themselves after a set time more
  • Disclosure: the statement that a post is an ad or gifted more
  • DM: direct message; a private message on a social app more
  • Doomscrolling: reading bad news for a long time more
  • DTR: define the relationship; the talk about what you are to each other more
  • Dude: a casual word for a man or friend more

E

  • Easy win: a match you won without trouble more
  • Engagement: likes, comments, shares and saves on a post more
  • EOD: end of day more
  • Epic: impressive, great more
  • Era: a phase of life with a theme more
  • ETA: estimated time of arrival; when you expect to get there more
  • Evergreen: content that stays useful for a long time more
  • Ex: a former partner more

F

  • Fake news: false information presented as news more
  • Fam: close friends or family more
  • FAQ: frequently asked questions more
  • Flame war: an angry online argument more
  • Flex: to show off, often money or things more
  • FOMO: fear of missing out; worry that others are doing something fun without you more
  • Forward: to send on a message you received more
  • Forwarded many times: a label on a message that has been passed along a lot more
  • Freelance: working for different clients without one employer more
  • Friend zone: being seen only as a friend by someone you like more
  • FTW: for the win; a cheer for something good more
  • Funda: idea or principle (from "fundamental") more
  • FYI: for your information; a note that needs no reply more

G

  • GG: good game; said at the end of a match more
  • Ghosted: stopped replying without any explanation more
  • Ghosting: cutting off contact without any explanation more
  • Gig: a short job or project, often freelance more
  • Glow up: a big positive change in looks or life more
  • GM / GN: good morning; good night more
  • GOAT: greatest of all time; high praise for a person more
  • Good morning spam: a flood of morning greetings in groups more
  • Green flag: a good sign about a person's behaviour more
  • Grind: to repeat the same task to level up more

H

  • Handle: your username on an app more
  • Hang out: to spend time together casually more
  • Hard launch: openly showing a partner for the first time more
  • Hashtag: a word with # that groups posts on a topic more
  • Heads up: a warning given in advance more
  • Highkey: very much; openly more
  • Hit the books: to study hard more
  • HMU: hit me up; contact me more
  • Ho jayega: it will be done more
  • Hook: the first moments or line meant to hold attention more
  • Hyped: very excited more

I

  • Ick: a sudden feeling of dislike for something small about a person more
  • Ick: a sudden loss of attraction because of a small thing more
  • ICYMI: in case you missed it; used when posting something again more
  • IDK: I do not know more
  • IMHO: in my humble opinion; a polite way to share a view more
  • IMO: in my opinion; a softer way to give a view more
  • Impressions: the number of times your post was shown, including repeats more
  • Influencer: a person who has a following and works with brands more
  • IRL: in real life; as opposed to online more

J

  • Jaldi: quickly more
  • Join link: an address that lets people enter a group more
  • JOMO: joy of missing out; being glad you skipped something more
  • Jugaad: a clever, cheap fix made with what you have more

K

  • Kab aa rahe ho: when are you coming more
  • Kal milte hain: let us meet tomorrow more
  • Kitna time lagega: how long will it take more
  • KPI: key performance indicator; a number that measures how well you are doing more
  • Kuch nahi: nothing; sometimes means more than it says more
  • Kya baat hai: wow, great; also asking "what is the matter" more

L

  • Lafda: a hassle or a fight more
  • Lag: delay caused by a slow connection more
  • Left on read: seeing a message and not replying more
  • Left on seen: seeing a message and not replying more
  • Legit: genuine, real more
  • LMK: let me know more
  • LOL: laughing out loud; in practice it often just means "that is funny" more
  • Loot: items you pick up in a game more
  • Love bombing: overwhelming someone with attention to gain control more
  • Lowkey: quietly or slightly; not openly more
  • Lurk: to read a group without posting more
  • Lurker: a member who reads but rarely writes more

M

  • Main character: someone acting as if life were a film about them more
  • Mast: great, excellent more
  • Maza aa gaya: I enjoyed it a lot more
  • Meme: an image, video or phrase that spreads and is copied with changes more
  • Mention: to tag a person in a message with @ more
  • Meta: the strategy most players currently use more
  • Mid: average, not good, not bad more
  • Monetise: to earn money from content more
  • Mood off hai: I am not in a good mood more
  • Mute: to stop notifications from a chat more

N

  • Nailed it: did something perfectly more
  • Nerf: to make something weaker in a game update more
  • Networking: meeting people to find opportunities more
  • Niche: a narrow subject you focus on more
  • No cap: no lie; used to say you are serious more
  • Noob: a beginner; can be friendly or insulting more
  • NP: no problem; a reply to thanks more
  • NSFW: not safe for work; warns that content is not suitable at a workplace more
  • NVM: never mind; forget what I asked more

O

  • OMW: on my way; you have left and are coming more
  • Onboarding: the process of a new person joining a team more
  • OOO: out of office more
  • OOTD: outfit of the day; a photo of what you are wearing more
  • OP: original poster; the person who started the thread more
  • OP: overpowered; too strong to be fair more

P

  • Paka mat: do not bore me more
  • Pakka: sure, definite more
  • Passive income: money that comes without daily work, after setup more
  • Pata nahi: I do not know more
  • Pin: to keep a chat or message at the top more
  • Ping: to send a quick message more
  • Pitch: a short presentation to win work or money more
  • Plus one: a guest brought along more
  • Poll: a question the group votes on more
  • POV: point of view; used to set up a short scene in a video or caption more

Q

  • Quiet quitting: doing only what the job requires, nothing extra more

R

  • Rage quit: to leave a game in anger more
  • Ratio: when replies to a post outnumber its likes, showing people disagree more
  • Reach: the number of different people who saw your post more
  • Reaction: an emoji response to a message more
  • Read receipt: the blue ticks that show a message was read more
  • Rebound: a relationship soon after a breakup more
  • Red flag: a warning sign about a person's behaviour more
  • Reels: short vertical videos on some apps more
  • Repurpose: to reuse one idea in a different format more
  • Respawn: to come back to life in the game more
  • Rizz: charm; the ability to attract or win over someone more
  • Rizz: charm in attracting someone more
  • RN: right now more
  • RT: retweet or repost; sharing someone else's post more

S

  • Sab theek: all fine more
  • Sahi hai: that is right, good more
  • Scene: situation or plan more
  • Set hai: it is arranged, all fine more
  • Shadowban: a belief that an app is quietly limiting a post's reach more
  • Side hustle: extra work done for money alongside a main job more
  • Simp: someone who does too much for a person who does not return it more
  • Situationship: a relationship with no clear label or commitment more
  • Slay: to do something very well or look great more
  • Slow fade: gradually reducing contact more
  • SMH: shaking my head; mild disbelief or disappointment more
  • Smurf: an expert playing on a new account against beginners more
  • Soft launch: hinting at a new partner in a post without showing them more
  • Spam: unwanted repeated messages more
  • Sponsored: a post paid for by a brand more
  • Squad: your team more
  • Stan: to support someone intensely, usually a celebrity more
  • Status: a post that disappears after a day, visible to your contacts more
  • Story: a post that disappears after a day more
  • Sus: suspicious; something seems wrong more

T

  • Talking stage: the early period of getting to know someone more
  • TBH: to be honest; you are about to say what you really think more
  • TBT: throwback Thursday; an old photo posted on a Thursday more
  • Tension mat le: do not worry more
  • Thak gaya: I am tired more
  • Theek hai: okay, fine more
  • Thoda wait: wait a little more
  • Thumbnail: the small picture that represents a video more
  • THX: thanks more
  • Tilt: losing calm and playing badly after a setback more
  • Timepass: passing time without a purpose more
  • TL;DR: too long, did not read; a short summary of a long post more
  • TMI: too much information; said when someone shares more than you wanted more
  • Touch grass: go outside; said to someone too absorbed in the internet more
  • Trend: a sound, format or topic many people are making posts about more
  • Trending: being talked about by many people right now more
  • Troll: a person who posts to annoy others more
  • Troll farm: a group paid to post and push opinions more
  • TTYL: talk to you later; a goodbye in chat more

U

  • Understood the assignment: did exactly what was needed, very well more
  • Upskill: learn new skills for work more

V

  • Vibe: the mood or feeling of a person, place or thing more
  • View once: a photo or video that can be opened a single time more
  • Viral: spreading fast across many people more
  • Voice note: a recorded audio message more

W

  • W / L: win / loss more
  • WFH: work from home more

Y

  • Yaar: friend, buddy; also used to add warmth or frustration more
  • YOLO: you only live once; an excuse to try something more
