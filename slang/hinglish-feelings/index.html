Hinglish for feelings and plans

How people say what they feel and what they plan in Roman Hindi.

When people want to say how they feel, they often switch to Hindi. A short phrase carries a feeling that an English sentence would take longer to say. These are the phrases you will see most in chats and comments.

Match the tone of the person you are writing to. A phrase that is friendly with a friend may be too casual for an elder.

  • Mood off hai

    I am not in a good mood.

    Example: "mood off hai, talk later"

  • Dil khush ho gaya

    That made me very happy.

    Example: "food was great, dil khush ho gaya"

  • Bore ho raha hoon

    I am bored.

    Example: "bore ho raha hoon, call me"

  • Tension mat le

    Do not worry.

    Example: "tension mat le, it will be fine"

  • Maza aa gaya

    I enjoyed it a lot.

    Example: "trip mein maza aa gaya"

  • Kya baat hai

    Wow, great; also asking "what is the matter".

    Example: "kya baat hai, new phone!"

  • Thak gaya

    I am tired.

    Example: "thak gaya, going to sleep"

  • Pata nahi

    I do not know.

    Example: "pata nahi, you decide"

  • Kuch nahi

    Nothing; sometimes means more than it says.

    Example: "kuch nahi, just thinking"

  • Chal raha hai

    It is going on, so-so.

    Example: "work chal raha hai"

  • Dekhte hain

    We will see; often a polite maybe.

    Example: "dekhte hain, I will tell you"

  • Ho jayega

    It will be done.

    Example: "ho jayega, do not worry"

  • Kitna time lagega

    How long will it take.

    Example: "kitna time lagega?"

  • Aaj nahi

    Not today.

    Example: "party aaj nahi"

  • Kal milte hain

    Let us meet tomorrow.

    Example: "okay, kal milte hain"

  • Sab theek

    All fine.

    Example: "sab theek at home"

  • Bas yahin

    Just here, nearby.

    Example: "bas yahin hoon"

  • Aaj kal

    These days.

    Example: "aaj kal busy hoon"

  • Kab aa rahe ho

    When are you coming.

    Example: "kab aa rahe ho?"

  • Thoda wait

    Wait a little.

    Example: "thoda wait, coming"

Questions people ask

Do these words mean the same everywhere?

Mostly, but not always. Slang changes with the group, the app and the year. Use the meaning here as the usual one and check with the context.

Why is a word not listed?

We list words that are common and not mainly abusive. Tell us on the contact page if you want one added.
